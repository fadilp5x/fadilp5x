I'm an **AI Creative Artist** and **Generative AI Video Producer** from Kerala, India — I direct AI ad films, microdramas and UGC campaigns for brands, and I build the software side of my world with code.

- 🔥 A jewellery film I made for **Rikka Joias** crossed **28.1M views**
- 🌍 Brand & client work across **India, UAE, Mozambique and Singapore**
- 🤖 Built Telegram bots used by **200k+ people**
- 🎓 BSc Computer Science student at **University of the People** · B.A. at Shamsul Huda Islamic Academy
- 🛠️ My AI film stack: **Veo 3.1 · Kling · Seedance · Higgsfield · ElevenLabs · Suno**
- 💻 My code stack: **Python · JavaScript · Node.js · Supabase · Firebase**
